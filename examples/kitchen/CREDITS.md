# Cozy Kitchen

“Cozy Kitchen” (Blender 3.5 splash scene) by
[Nicole Morena](https://www.artstation.com/nickyblender),
[source .blend](https://download.blender.org/demo/splash/blender-3.5-splash.blend),
[CC BY-SA](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/) (version unspecified by source).
No artist endorsement implied. The source scene and adaptations, including
rendered images and animations, retain CC BY-SA; the example code's licence
does not replace the asset licence.

This preparation changes the camera to a seven-second orbit, the framing to
widescreen, and exports geometry through the Blender bridge. It reuses a
shared procedural material program, source colour palettes and wood
colour-chain restoration. Noise differs and some bump/normal detail and complex
closure mixtures are omitted. Retain these changes and the export report with
distribution. The `look/` presets, palettes and material program form part of this
CC BY-SA scene adaptation, independently of the repository's code licence.

The animation behind the README image also used animated lighting, staging changes, a larger
backdrop, reduced visibility of offstage plants and stand/trunk meshes, and a
smaller fill-light radius.
Its material translation omitted some bump detail and used different noise.
Those further staging and animated-light changes are not reproduced by this recipe.
That adapted animation is shared under CC BY-SA.
