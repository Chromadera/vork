cloth: GPU XPBD cloth on the ordinary geometry compute chain.
Python: from vork.cloth import attach; attach(scene, "fabric", collider="body").
Call before write_scene, on native triangle buffers in world metres. Pins are
vertex indices; collider names a native mesh, including animated skin or morphs.
Scenes without cloth install no cloth resources or dispatches.
Use vork help cloth --all for controls and limits.

# Cloth simulation

attach(scene, node, *, pins=(), collider=None, dt=None, iterations=64,
       density=.2, stretch_modulus=50000, bending_rigidity=.002, damping=.8,
       collision_margin=.004, acceleration=None)

The helper appends ordinary compute programs and state to scene.simulation and
rewires POSITION/NORMAL/TANGENT, preserving surface materials, UVs and other
attributes. Each object receives automatic private state, reset, checkpoints
and canonical predecessor inputs. Existing passes keep their order. Optional
Slang includes share generated typed accessors: cloth/solver.slang,
cloth/body.slang, cloth/self.slang. No renderer cloth primitive or extra clock.

Units: density kg/m², stretch modulus N/m, bending rigidity N·m, damping 1/s,
margin metres. Mass uses triangle area; stretch/dihedral compliance uses dual
edge widths. XPBD accumulates multipliers within each tick, with compliance/dt².
Reference: https://matthias-research.github.io/pages/publications/XPBD.pdf
The finite iterative solve still needs convergence checks for the chosen mesh.

Omitted dt inherits scene.simulation.dt, otherwise 1/240 seconds. All patches
share that exact clock. iterations is positive and even. Pins follow canonical
predecessor positions, so native morphs/skin can animate attachments.
acceleration is a Slang body with i, p, v and time, returning float3 in m/s²
INCLUDING gravity. Default: return float3(0,-9.81,0);. A wind-like loading:
return float3(20,-9.81,4*sin(time*3+p.x*4))-1.5*v;
This prescribes acceleration, not calibrated aerodynamic pressure.

Use consistently oriented fixed native triangles, 3..1024 vertices per patch,
no unused vertices, degenerate triangles or nonmanifold edges. Bake transforms
into rest vertices and keep each cloth node at an identity root; animate pins
through skin/morphs instead of root TRS. Instances, imported geometry,
subdivision and computed colliders need a baked native proxy. A collider may
have 1..4096 triangles and authored NORMAL (split triangle vertices for face
normals); the general Geometry input samples it at canonical tick
left endpoints. Keep motion per tick small relative to the collision margin.

Surface contact uses point/face and edge/edge constraints, Coulomb friction .6
and self separation 6*collision_margin (three margins on each surface).
Contact generation runs in parallel;
contact planes refresh up to eight times per tick; colored structural constraints
and ordered contact projection share one
workgroup. There are 512 contact slots per vertex. Overflow invalidates output
rather than silently dropping contacts. On invalid computed positions, check
contact density, initial intersections, dt and numerical scale; separate crowded
patches or simplify the collision proxy. No continuous collision detection:
fast motion or extreme folding needs smaller dt and convergence checks.

Publication reconstructs area-weighted normals and UV0 tangents/handedness;
without UV0 it supplies a finite geometric frame. Duplicated seam vertices are
independent unless connected by triangles. State stays on GPU; no runtime
position readback. Cost grows with contacts and iterations; the contact-heavy
625-vertex drape is a quality example, not an interactive performance promise.
